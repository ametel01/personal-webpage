## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is engineering managers evaluating Alex Metelli for serious remote software engineering opportunities. Recruiters are the secondary audience, followed by technical peers.

Visitors need to determine quickly whether Alex can take ambiguous engineering problems, structure them, build reliable systems, document the work, and ship. They should be able to inspect concrete projects and evidence rather than rely on generic portfolio claims.

## Product Purpose

This personal website is a professional proof surface for Alex's software engineering work. It exists to earn remote engineering roles and selected consulting engagements with concrete technical scope.

Success means a qualified visitor understands Alex's capabilities in backend and platform engineering, developer infrastructure, local-first AI tooling and agent workflows, and blockchain systems, then reviews the supporting work, resume, or contact paths.

## Positioning

Alex is a backend/platform engineer building local-first AI tooling, developer infrastructure, and blockchain systems where provenance, observability, and correctness matter.

AI-assisted software-development tooling is part of the lead position rather than a secondary footnote. Blockchain experience remains substantive depth without defining Alex as blockchain-only.

The site differentiates through concrete, reviewable engineering evidence: professional work at Nethermind, public developer tools, live products, protocol experiments, case studies, and source repositories. It must not depend on inflated claims or fabricated metrics.

## Operating Context

Visitors evaluate Alex through the homepage, a curated work index, seven project case studies, an about page, a structured web resume, and an optional downloadable PDF resume. Contact paths appear within existing pages rather than on a dedicated contact page.

External evidence includes public repositories, release artifacts, live products, documentation, deployed protocol information, and prior work maintained under Nethermind. The site supports remote and asynchronous opportunities and communicates timezone flexibility without publishing exact geography.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with engineering proof: make substantial work and verifiable evidence easier to assess than self-description.
2. Establish breadth without blurring the position: backend/platform engineering and developer infrastructure lead, with meaningful depth in AI tooling and blockchain systems.
3. Optimize for trust: keep claims restrained, current, specific, and supported by real artifacts.
4. Respect the evaluator's time: help engineering managers understand fit, depth, and availability quickly, with clear paths to deeper evidence.
5. Preserve professional privacy: communicate remote readiness and flexibility without publishing unnecessary personal details.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site must use semantic HTML, maintain one `h1` per page, provide visible keyboard focus, preserve meaningful labels beyond icons, support keyboard navigation, meet at least WCAG 2.1 AA contrast for normal text, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
